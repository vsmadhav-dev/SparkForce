About Us

SparkForce is an app designed for workers such as lumberjacks, electricians, and carpenters who have limited networks and want to find jobs. It solves this problem by connecting them with people who need their services, providing details such as location, scope, budget, and deadline.

In this system, a registered job poster posts a job. Workers (called fixers) who are available in the nearby network can search for jobs and see the posted listing along with the contact number. After the work is completed, the job poster can mark the job as finished and record the worker’s ID.

Our aim is to help people who need this system—these fixers and job posters—and who have a limited network, all free of charge.
